Pitch Please

Sell silly stuff to ridiculous AI characters, out loud. Don't get hung up on.

How to play

Pick a caller and read your goal. Tap TALK and pitch out loud, then pause for a moment (or tap DONE). Raise Trust and the Deal meter to 100% before Suspicion fills up or Patience runs out. Win calls to earn coins and unlock new callers.

Why does it say "Demo mode"?

Unscripted calls use Apple Intelligence, which needs a compatible iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on (Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri). Other iPhones can play every call with pre-written replies.

The character can't hear me

Check Settings → Pitch Please and turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition. You can also tap the keyboard button during a call and type instead.

Is this a real phone call?

No. Every call and character is a fictional simulation inside the app. Pitch Please never places phone calls, sends messages or connects you to other people.
