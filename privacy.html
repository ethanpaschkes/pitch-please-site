Pitch Please

Privacy Policy · Last updated October 17, 2026

The short version

Pitch Please does not collect, store or share your personal data. There is no account, no server, no ads and no tracking.

Microphone and speech

The microphone is used only while you are on a pretend call, so you can talk to the fictional character. Your speech is turned into text using Apple's Speech framework, on your iPhone when your iPhone supports on-device recognition. The app uses that text only to write the character's reply during the call. Pitch Please does not save recordings or send audio anywhere itself.

You can turn the microphone or speech recognition off at any time in Settings and type your pitch instead.

AI characters

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, character replies are written by Apple's on-device language model (Apple Foundation Models). On other iPhones, replies come from pre-written demo lines. Pitch Please uses no other AI service.

Data stored on your iPhone

Your coins, unlocked callers and best scores are saved only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

Pitch Please does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

It's all pretend

All calls and characters are fictional simulations. Pitch Please never places real phone calls, sends messages or connects you to other people.
